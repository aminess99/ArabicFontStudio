---
name: Localized download flow
description: User-approved navigation and SEO expectations for English and French font detail pages
---

English and French font-list buttons should open the corresponding font detail page in the visitor’s language, with actual file download available there. Use “View & Download” and “Voir et télécharger” for the navigation buttons.

Localized font detail pages should provide a substantive, translated equivalent of the Arabic detail page, with font-specific information and useful content available to both visitors and search crawlers. Preserve original font names, license terms, and source notes rather than inventing translations for them.

**Why:** The user requested the same details-before-download experience they expect from the Arabic site and believes complete localized pages can improve SEO in other languages.

**How to apply:** Preserve this flow in directory, category, and collection listings. Keep the final detail-page download direct, and synchronize visible localized content with crawler-facing HTML and page metadata.
