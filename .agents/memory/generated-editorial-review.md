---
name: Generated editorial review
description: Publication policy for automatically generated editorial content
---

Automatically generated blog content must fail closed: generation creates a private draft, never a public article. Publication requires both an automated quality pass and an explicit human review covering originality, facts, sources, dates, licensing, and real font references.

**Why:** Syntax and model-authored citations cannot establish factual accuracy, source support, licensing rights, or meaningful information gain. Human verification is required before search indexing.

**How to apply:** Keep all public blog surfaces behind one publication predicate. For localized articles, preserve executable code, URLs, and literal examples while translating human-readable comments; review facts and language before indexing. Automation must not bypass editorial approval or recycle exhausted topics.
